| file | title | caption |
|---|---|---|
| P1_overview | Every setting is read off the file | Flow of the per-file estimation: the file's scan times and intensity floor, the 2,000 strongest distinct ions as seeds, the four quantities measured on the seed traces, and the settings derived from them. Numbers from one real HZV029 injection (batch4_MT_20210729_003C.mzML). |
| P2_seeds_and_peak_width | Seeds and the peak width | (a) The seeds: the 2,000 most intense centroids that are mutually ≥ 20 ppm apart, one per strong ion, at its apex (orange ring: the seed of b). (b) For each seed the EIC (max per scan within ± 20 ppm) around the apex is averaged over 3 scans and its width at half maximum read off by interpolation; seeds without a crossing on both sides or fewer than 3 raw scans above half max are skipped. (c) The median width of all measurable seeds sets how many adjacent scans are linked (K = half the width in scans, 1–3), the minimum number of scans of a peak (3–5) and the smoothing kernel (half the median width). |
| P3_mz_error_model | The m/z error model, fitted per file | (a) Along one seed's trace the centroid m/z scatters little at the apex and more in the tails: the error depends on intensity. (b) All centroids of the 2,000 seed traces, as deviation from each ion's intensity-weighted mean m/z. Per half-decade of intensity a robust sd (1.4826 × MAD) is taken and sd² = a² + b²/I fitted by least squares (a ≥ 0.3 ppm). (c) At linking time two centroids may be the same ion when their m/z distance is within the tolerance their own intensities allow, capped at 4 σ of the file's floor intensity (5–30 ppm). |
| P4_noise_law | The scan-to-scan noise law, fitted per file | (a) On every seed trace (the contiguous scans ≥ 10 % of the apex) each point is compared with the local quadratic through its neighbours; the residuals are noise, not peak shape (apex excluded, scaled by √(35/18) to undo the fit's variance reduction). (b) The robust sd of the residuals per half-decade of level and the law σ(I)² = floor² + c·I + r²·I² fitted to the bins by non-negative least squares. (c) The law gives the expected noise at any level: a candidate's shape descriptors (its significant valleys, its total variation beyond one rise and fall) are counted in units of it, so the same dip is ripple on a weak ion and a real valley on a strong one. |
| P5_scan_gains | Per-scan gain, read off the strongest ions | A gain all ions of a scan share (AGC / injection-time error, spray flicker) is estimated from the seed traces: each point's log intensity minus the quadratic through its four neighbours in time is that scan's log gain minus the neighbours'; the median over the traces covering a scan gives one equation per scan and all gains are solved at once with a ridge set by the spread actually seen, so a file without jitter beyond its noise gets gains of 1 (the HZV029 demo file does). Shown on an SZ22 injection, the replicate-run file with the largest gain sd. Every scan is divided by its gain before picking; the floor and the noise law are then measured again. |
| P6_what_the_files_said | What the files said: measured settings across the benchmark | The per-file sidecars of the current default run (113 files in 16 datasets): scan interval, median peak width in seconds and in scans, the m/z tolerance cap, the intensity floor and the scan-gain sd. They span 0.2–1.4 s per scan, 1.7–11 s peak widths, 5–30 ppm and two orders of magnitude in floor between Orbitrap and Q-TOF files; no single setting would fit them all, and none was chosen by hand. Star: the HZV029 injection of the worked example. |
